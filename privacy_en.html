FoodSnap AI – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Protecting your data matters to us. This page explains what data FoodSnap AI processes, where it is sent and what rights you have. FoodSnap AI does not require a user account.

1. Controller

emredevelopstudio
Email: emredevelop.studio@gmail.com

2. Photo analysis with artificial intelligence (Google Gemini)

When you take a photo of a meal or choose an image from your gallery, the photo is resized and sent encrypted (HTTPS) to Google's Gemini API. It is analyzed there to identify the dish and estimate its nutritional values. The result is sent back to the app.

  • Recipient: Google LLC or Google Ireland Ltd. (Gemini API). Processing on servers outside the EU, in particular in the USA, is possible.
  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you give before your first scan. Without consent, the AI scan stays disabled.
  • Withdrawal: possible at any time in the app settings.
  • Please do not photograph people or sensitive documents.
  • Information from Google: Gemini API Terms and Google Privacy Policy.

3. Data stored locally

Your meals, photos, goals, profile (e.g. weight, height) and fasting data are stored only locally on your device. We do not operate our own server where this data is stored.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads via Google AdMob. In the process, device identifiers (e.g. the advertising ID), IP address and usage data may be sent to Google. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent through Google's consent dialog before any ads are loaded; without consent, no ads are loaded. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings > Ad settings. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. More information: How Google uses data for advertising. FoodSnap AI Pro shows no ads.

5. In-app purchases (Google Play & RevenueCat)

Purchases are processed via Google Play. To verify and restore purchases, we use RevenueCat, Inc. (USA). This involves an anonymous app user ID and purchase information, but no payment details. More information: RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, and to withdraw your consent with effect for the future. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

8. Data deletion (Data Deletion Request)

Because your app data is stored only on your device, you can delete it completely at any time yourself: Settings > Apps > FoodSnap AI > Storage > Clear data, or by uninstalling the app.

For questions or deletion requests regarding data held by third parties, write to:
emredevelop.studio@gmail.com
with the subject: "Data deletion FoodSnap AI".
